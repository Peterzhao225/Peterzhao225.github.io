Hi! I am **Jiale Zhao**, an undergraduate student at the School of Automation Science and Electrical Engineering, **Beihang University**, advised by [Prof. Liyuanjun Lai](https://teacher.buaa.edu.cn/lailiyuanjun/).

My research interests lie in **reinforcement learning**, **embodied intelligence**, and **operations research**. I am interested in building learning and decision-making systems that can interact with complex environments, discover effective strategies, and solve challenging optimization problems.

### Research Interests

* **Reinforcement Learning:** decision-making, policy learning, and agentic systems
* **Embodied Intelligence:** perception, interaction, and intelligent agents in physical environments
* **Operations Research:** combinatorial optimization, scheduling, and routing
