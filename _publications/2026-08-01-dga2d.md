**Jiale Zhao**, Zimu Chen, Sirui Mao, Wentao Yang, Yuxiang Bai, and Liyuanjun Lai.

DGA2D represents an open-ended program space as a directed graph: nodes are functional operators with alternative code implementations, while directed walks form complete algorithmic pipelines. A path-dependent credit assignment mechanism evaluates code variations within their topological context.

Across 12 combinatorial optimization problems spanning scheduling and routing, DGA2D reduces the average normalized gap by up to 10.96 percentage points compared with state-of-the-art LLM-based baselines.
