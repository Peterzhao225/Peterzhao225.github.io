**Jiale Zhao**, Sirui Mao, Zimu Chen, Wentao Yang, Zihan Wang, Xuefeng Huang, Junji Cheng, and Liyuanjun Lai.

Just Initialize shifts the focus of large-scale routing optimization from increasingly expensive refinement procedures to the quality of the initial solution. The method compresses a large routing instance into a compact surrogate space, optimizes its global routing structure, and recovers an optimization-friendly starting point in the original space.

Experiments cover TSP, CVRP, VRPTW, and PCTSP instances ranging from 1K to 100K nodes. The approach achieves solution quality comparable to or better than state-of-the-art methods while delivering an average speedup of approximately 70 times.
